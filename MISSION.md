# The Provome Mission

## Why we exist

The world now reads DNA faster than it can understand it. Every day,
labs, doctors, veterinarians and farmers produce answers-shaped data
with no verified answer inside: variants nobody can interpret,
diseases nobody can name, targets nobody has proven. People wait
years inside that gap.

Provome exists to close it.

## What we do

Provome is an evidence company. For any biological problem, in any
species, our Copilot returns one of two things: an answer backed by
verified evidence, with a source a human can check for every claim,
or an honest decline that names exactly what evidence is missing.
Never a guess dressed as an answer.

## Our divisions

- Human: every disease, not only cancer. Rare disease first, because
  that is where the waiting is longest and our benchmark is proven.
- Animal: veterinary and livestock health, animal DNA, animal
  disease.
- Plant: crops, plant disease, breeding, food security.
- Drug Discovery: computational target evidence, repurposing
  evidence and candidate prioritisation. The brain of drug
  discovery, in software, before any lab spends a dollar.

## Our law

Cite, or decline. Every claim carries its evidence. Every result
passes independent cross-checks and staged quality control before
it is allowed to be an answer. When the evidence is not there, we
say so, in plain words, and we say what would change our mind.

## How we work

One Copilot on the outside. An organisation of senior specialists
inside, checking each other the way a great company checks its own
work. Production standard or it does not ship. Free for students to
learn on, premium for the professionals whose work depends on it,
friendly enough for daily use by anyone.

## Where we are going

In three to four months: the full platform, three divisions live on
one engine, drug discovery in software, used daily by students,
professors, researchers, labs and companies. After that: the
physical and clinical world, through licensed partners, one proven
step at a time.

We are not the pill. We are the reason the right pill, the right
diagnosis and the right decision can exist: the evidence,
verified.
