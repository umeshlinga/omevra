An evidence engine for biology

Proof, or an honest decline.

Ask Provome about a gene and a variant. It fetches real evidence from the world's scientific databases, checks it through a cite-or-decline gate, and answers only when at least three verified items from two independent sources support the answer — each one named, with its fetch date. When the evidence is not there, Provome declines and tells you exactly what is missing. It never guesses.

How it works

One Copilot speaks to you. Behind it, specialist workers gather evidence, cross-check each other, and pass every result through quality stages and a senior review. Every run leaves an audit trail — and the Copilot shows its work.

1 · You ask

A gene and a variant, in plain language: “What is known about CFTR p.Phe508del?” The Copilot recognises the topic — or declines immediately if it cannot.

2 · It fetches

Live records from ClinVar, population frequencies from gnomAD when coordinates allow, and peer-reviewed literature via Europe PMC. Nothing is answered from memory.

3 · The gate decides

Three or more verified items, two or more independent sources, no unresolved refutation — or the answer is a decline with the exact reasons. The gate cannot be talked out of it.

On the roadmap

Drug Discovery

Computational only: target evidence, variant-to-target reasoning, repurposing candidates. A patient-ready drug still needs synthesis, lab and animal testing, human trials, and regulatory approval through licensed partners — Provome never claims otherwise.
